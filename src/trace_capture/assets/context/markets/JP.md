# Japan starter context

- Write captions and all visible schedule text in natural Japanese.
- Prefer a concrete commute, class, club, part-time work, or errand sequence over a broad productivity claim.
- Keep the first line short and conversational; avoid excessive emoji, hashtags, and exaggerated superlatives.
- Seasonal or annual events may be used only when the selected persona situation names them.
- Reference `jp-campus-commute` for a student day and `jp-office-errands` for a working day.
