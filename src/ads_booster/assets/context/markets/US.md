# United States starter context

- Write captions and all visible schedule text in concise, natural US English.
- Show one specific routine such as class blocks, shifts, appointments, commuting, practice, or household errands.
- Lead with a believable first-person observation; avoid hype, fabricated outcomes, and generic productivity slogans.
- Do not assume a city, school calendar, holiday, or weather unless the selected persona situation supplies it.
- Reference `us-campus-shift` for a student day and `us-hybrid-workday` for a working day.
