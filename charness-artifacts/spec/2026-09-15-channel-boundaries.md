# Channel boundary repair contract
Status: Implemented candidate; not deployed. Issue: #186.

An authenticated request owns every resumed slice. Revocation is checked on each execution.
Independent new work does not retry uncertain effects; original late results retain delivery ownership.
Completion receives scoped facts and exact evidence IDs. Artifact digest/owner checks remain required;
ordinary generation and already-persisted memory require no invented approval/review/write.
A new observation may refresh input while persisted invocation recovery remains idempotent.

Acceptance: the focused installed tests and actual-provider results in the dated channel audit.
Non-claims: no production activation, direct Threads access, general account scheduler or full visual certification.
